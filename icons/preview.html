Round 4 — fixing what's wrong with the shipped icon (7d)

The current mark reads as Angular at small sizes: hexagon ring + inner letter is too busy, the inner ring vanishes below 32px, and the N is wider than tall. Three directions that address that — increasing departure from the current silhouette.

11 · Refined shield · geometric N (drops the inner ring)

Same shield silhouette as today, but the inner hexagonal frame is gone — just a white shield with a dark N. The N is geometrically constructed: 12px verticals, 10px diagonal (optically balanced), taller-than-wide proportions. Should survive 16px.
